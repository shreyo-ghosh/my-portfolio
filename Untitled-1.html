Sacred Attractions

Discover the most revered sites that make Varanasi the spiritual capital of India

Dashashwamedh Ghat

The most famous ghat where the spectacular Ganga Aarti ceremony takes place every evening, drawing thousands of devotees and visitors.

🕕 Evening Aarti: 6:30 PM

Kashi Vishwanath Temple

One of the twelve Jyotirlingas, this golden temple dedicated to Lord Shiva is the most sacred shrine in Varanasi.

⏰ Open: 4:00 AM - 11:00 PM

Sarnath

The deer park where Buddha gave his first sermon after enlightenment. A UNESCO World Heritage site with ancient stupas and monasteries.

📍 10 km from Varanasi

Manikarnika Ghat

The main cremation ghat where Hindus believe cremation leads to moksha (liberation from the cycle of rebirth).

🕉️ Sacred cremation site

Assi Ghat

A peaceful ghat popular with pilgrims and tourists, perfect for morning boat rides and witnessing beautiful sunrises over the Ganges.

🛶 Best for sunrise views

Banaras Hindu University

One of India's largest residential universities, home to the beautiful Vishwanath Temple and sprawling campus with rich academic heritage.

📚 Est. 1916

Spiritual Experiences

Immerse yourself in the timeless traditions and sacred rituals of Varanasi

🛶

Sunrise Boat Ride

Experience the magic of Varanasi from the sacred waters of the Ganges. Watch the city wake up as pilgrims perform their morning rituals, and witness the golden sunrise painting the ancient ghats in ethereal light.

⏰ Best time: 5:30 AM - 7:00 AM
💰 Cost: ₹200-500 per person
📍 Starting point: Dashashwamedh Ghat
🪔

Ganga Aarti Ceremony

Witness the mesmerizing evening prayer ceremony where priests perform synchronized rituals with fire, incense, and chanting. This daily spectacle draws thousands of devotees and creates an unforgettable spiritual atmosphere.

⏰ Daily: 6:30 PM - 7:30 PM
🎭 Duration: 45 minutes
📍 Location: Dashashwamedh Ghat
