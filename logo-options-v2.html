What top studios do (applied here)

  • Pentagram (Oxide, Tractable, Graphcore, MIND, Secondmind) — mark encodes product metaphor; black as technical canvas; one hot accent; motion-ready.
  • Saffron (AlphaSense, Bittensor) — radical simplicity + quiet confidence; systems that scale from favicon to campaign.
  • Smith & Diction (Perplexity) — bake the differentiator into the symbol (their asterisk = citations).
  • Vercel / Linear habit — extreme constraint; passes favicon + single-color + motion tests.
  • For LaunchLayer — encode launch through layers (ship to prod), amber as voltage, teal as reliability, void as focus. Avoid rockets, clouds, purple AI glow, white squares.
